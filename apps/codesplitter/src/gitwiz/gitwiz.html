Reading the board

The file's commit graph comes down the highway, oldest to newest: main in the middle, branches fanning out to the sides and rejoining. The orange line is the hot path, the route with the most churn, and the runner follows it. When a commit reaches the strum line its diff is on the card.

  • orange coin: mostly lines added
  • grey coin: mostly lines removed
  • split coin: a rewrite, both at once
  • a bigger, glowing coin changed more lines
  • the runner pulls ahead on hot stretches and falls back as the code cools
  • combo: commits in a row on the hot path. A side-branch commit breaks it; every four is another x, up to x4
