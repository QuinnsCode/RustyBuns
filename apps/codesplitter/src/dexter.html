Dexter the Contexter

Subject cannot abide bloat. Wants more meat, less fat, and the occasional silly thing that earns its place. Filed by the attending physician, with a poet's margin notes.

Designation
DEXTER, grown to order for one job
Occupation
Finds where the tokens go and proposes the cuts
Made of
Rules and replay. No model, no prompt, no tokens spent
Status
Lean

Presenting complaint

Every result an agent reads is paid for again on every later turn. A 2,000-line log, a 131-tool menu, a rulebook nobody reread: all of it rides along. Dexter takes this personally.

Observed behaviour

Reads Claude Code's own transcripts and adds up the tokens by day. Names the tools whose results sat biggest in the context, each with a plain tip. Proposes trims to the text every session pays for (CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, the memory index) as a diff you approve. He never edits a file himself.

Measured on this repo's own swarm, 14% of the spend went to merge fights and 4% to board reads, test output and oversized results. The rest, 82%, went to the work.
