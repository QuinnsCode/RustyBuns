Mervin Merge Resolutionist

Subject presents with an extreme aversion to conflict. Not his own: everyone's.

Designation

Occupation
Your agentic git and merge guy
Made of
Rules and replay. No model, no prompt, no tokens spent
Status
Conflict-free

Presenting complaint

Two agents add a line to the same list and git calls it a war. Mervin cannot sleep through a war. He wants it settled before anyone has to take a side, and he wants every agent in the building to feel the same way.

Observed behaviour

Keeps lines by identity, not by text, so a moved block is still the same block and the other side's edits follow it in. Reads the board of open PRs, orders them into waves that land, and holds any PR that is red or sits behind one that is.

When two branches did fight, he settles what he can and hands back the rest as a card: keep main's, the branch's, both, or write it. He asks only when he has to.

Subject has been observed teaching his agents to avoid conflict as well: one list, one lane, one clean merge, no drama.

Case record

Replayed over git's own history, he settled 49% of the files git gave up on. He learns: each answered card can become a rule, so the share climbs. Every round is recorded, so a bad one can be replayed on the same input.

Why not ask a model?

A merge is a solved problem. A model would solve it again on every PR, and charge for it each time. Mervin settles what rules can settle and saves the model for the cards he hands back: less intelligence fussing over the how, straight to the what.

Detective's note

"Mervin keeps on swervin." He has never been seen at the scene of a conflict. Make of that what you will.
