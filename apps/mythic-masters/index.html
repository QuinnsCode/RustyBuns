The Bog Witch

"You came down in the light, you and the loud one. Nobody believes you. I do. Five came to my door this year, out of the pines and the north woods, the desert and the high Alps. Take three. They walk with you to Leeds Point, and when you bind the others, I keep them for you, in this."

She holds up a book bound in cedar bark: the Eldritch Compendium.
