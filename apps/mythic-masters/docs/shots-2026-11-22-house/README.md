# The house look: the roster before, and three pilots, 2026-11-22

The round that wrote [ART-STYLE.md](../ART-STYLE.md) and the kit in `proposals/house/`. It stopped at the three
pilots: Blender no longer runs on the office Mac (the owner's order, 2026-11-23), so the rest of the roster waits.

## The roster before

`before-gallery-0.jpg` to `before-gallery-4.jpg`: every beast in the game's gallery on 2026-11-22
(`bun scripts/beasts.ts --shots --as before`). Three styles stand side by side:

- the chunky starters (Hodag, Squonk, Jackalope on page 0): the look to copy;
- lean, small-headed beasts built to real proportions (Wampus Cat, Rougarou, Jersey Devil);
- about fifty older sheets that are skin-modifier tubes with bead eyes (Kelpie, Mokele-mbembe, Camahueto on page 1).

Pages 1 and 2 hold the old Kelpie, Loch Ness Monster and Minotaur, the three this round rebuilt.

## The pilots

`lineup-pilots.jpg`: the Hodag, Jackalope and Squonk beside the rebuilt Kelpie, Minotaur and Loch Ness Monster, one
camera, true scale (`bun proposals/house/lineup.ts hodag jackalope squonk kelpie minotaur nessie`). The close-ups
(`kelpie-closeup.jpg`, `minotaur-closeup.jpg`, `nessie-closeup.jpg`, and `hodag-closeup.jpg` to compare) are front,
side, three-quarter and head.

What holds: one face on all three (white ball, coloured iris, round pupil, a catchlight), big heads, thick short
limbs, big feet. They read as one family with the starters.

What is not good enough yet (the lead's review, not a judge's):

1. The upper lid is a ring that reads as goggles, and on the far eye a grey slab and a white sliver show at the
   head's edge (the Kelpie's head close-up).
2. The default brows read worried on the Kelpie and the Loch Ness Monster.
3. The mouth is a thin dark-red line with no volume.
4. The Kelpie's and the Loch Ness Monster's heads are faceted.
5. The surface is the biggest gap: beside the Hodag's clumped fur and rows of spines, the Kelpie and the Loch Ness
   Monster look like smooth plastic. The Minotaur's fur is closer.
6. Hooves are grey wedges.

No score is claimed for this round. The owner has not seen it, and a judge was not run.
