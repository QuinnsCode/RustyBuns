# Hodag rebuild, 2026-11-10

The owner scored the old Hodag 1.3 out of 10 next to the references. Three rebuilds were tried (PR #4171):
a statue, the 1893 hoax and a big-head starter. The owner picked the statue, which is now `beasts/hodag.json`.

## What made it better, for the next starter

- The head is round flesh (two `blobs`, a cranium and a bulldog muzzle, merged with `blend`). The `skull` loft comes out as a box, and its `width` and `height` are half sizes.
- The grin is a dark `ridge` lip band with `horn` teeth placed on the muzzle's surface, plus two big fangs.
- The back spines are curved `horn`s that swell toward the hips, not straight `spikes` cones.
- Three hooked `horn` claws on each paw, instead of the paw's own small claws.
