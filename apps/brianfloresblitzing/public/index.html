One Team in 32

NFL blitz rate, 2026, through Week 10

One team in 32 can move the whole league average

Brian Flores runs the Vikings defense. He sends extra rushers more than any coach in the league.

Coach style: bring pressure. Make the quarterback guess. Do it again.

  1. A blitz is 5 or more rushers

    Most plays, a defense sends 4. A blitz sends 1 or more extra. Send one more than they can block.

  2. 72 of every 100 pass plays

    The Vikings blitz on 72.3% of pass plays. A typical team blitzes on about 31.

  3. No. 2 is not close

    Washington is second at 43.6%. The Vikings are almost 30 points ahead. Nobody else is close.

  4. One team is 1 vote in 32

    Each team counts once in the league average. That is 1 out of 32, or 3.1%.

  5. The average goes up 1.3 points

    One very high number pulls the league average up. Without the Vikings it is 30.7%. With them it is 32.0%.

  6. Past the old high

    Since 2006, no team blitzed more than 52.4% for a full season. That record is gone.

Blitz

Blitz rate

Percentage points, not game points. A team that blitzes 72% of the time and a team that blitzes 31% of the time are 41 percentage points apart. This is not about the scoreboard.

3% of the teams is not 3% of the blitzes

Each team counts once in the average. That is the 3%. But a team that blitzes more sends more blitzes. Count who sends them.
