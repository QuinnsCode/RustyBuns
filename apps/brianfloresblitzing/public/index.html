One team in 32 can move the whole league average

There are 32 teams. Each one counts once in the league average. That is 1 out of 32, or 3.1%.

The Vikings blitz way more than everyone else. So they drag the average up. By a lot.

Percentage points, not game points. A team that blitzes 70% of the time and a team that blitzes 30% of the time are 40 percentage points apart. Nothing to do with the scoreboard.
