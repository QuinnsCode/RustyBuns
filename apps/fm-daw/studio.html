The gear room

The live room. The amps are on the rack, the pedals hang on the pedal wall, the cab is on the floor, the guitar is on its stand. Cable the guitar into an amp (through pedals in a row, or two side by side on Q Split) and the amp into the cab, and you hear that circuit.

WASD walk, mouse look. Click a box to pick it up, click again to set it down, Q / E turn it. Click a jack to take a cable, walk it to another jack and click. Drag on a knob to turn it. Drop a tscircuit board's circuit JSON in the room and it stands on the floor as a pedal. Right-click or G lets go of a cable, M speakers, Esc lets go of the mouse.
