The gear room

The live room. The amps are on the rack, the pedals hang on the pedal wall, the cab is on the floor, the guitar is on its stand. Cable the guitar into an amp (through pedals in a row, or two side by side on Q Split) and the amp into the cab, and you hear that circuit.

Right of the rack is the sound wall, a modular case with a sequencer, an oscillator, a filter, a VCA, an envelope, an LFO, a mixer with two sends and returns, and an output to the room. Patch them, and cable a send through a pedal or Q Spring and back into its return. N brings another module to set into the case.

WASD walk, mouse look. Click a box to pick it up, click again to set it down, Q / E turn it. Click a jack to take a cable, walk it to another jack and click. Drag on a knob to turn it. Drop a tscircuit board's circuit JSON in the room and it stands on the floor as a pedal. B opens the build sheet of a pedal you look at or hold (its schematic, board, parts and a kit to download). N a new sound wall module, right-click or G lets go of a cable, M speakers, Tab the desk (a strip for you, sends to its returns: Space, or gear cabled between its aux-send and aux-return jacks), Esc lets go of the mouse.
