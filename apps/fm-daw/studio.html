Q Studio

The live room. The amps and pedals are on the rack, the cab is on the floor, the guitar is on its stand. Cable the guitar into an amp (through a pedal if you like) and the amp into the cab, and you hear that circuit.

WASD walk, mouse look. Click a box to pick it up, click again to set it down, Q / E turn it. Click a jack to take a cable, walk it to another jack and click. Drag on a knob to turn it. Right-click or G lets go of a cable, M speakers, Esc lets go of the mouse.
