        How it works

        Invite copies a link: anyone with it walks into this room, and eqosys friends get it in their notes.

        Sit at an instrument and your keys play its track for everyone. WASD walk (Shift runs), mouse look, Space hops. E or click sits; Space stands. Seated, A to ' are notes (Z / X octave), or a MIDI keyboard. V camera, P groove, Esc out.

        Plug in takes your computer's audio input: a guitar goes through the amp at your seat (the bass, else the first track with an amp); in the vocal booth, your mic goes straight to everyone. Wear headphones.

        Mutes are yours alone; the leader can mute someone for everyone.
