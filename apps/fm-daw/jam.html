The live room: the kit, the keys and a bass rig, a vocal booth in the corner, and the desk behind the control room's glass. Walk up to an instrument and sit, and your keys play its track in the sesh, so everyone in the room hears it.

WASD walk, mouse look. Space or click sits you at the spot in front of you, and again stands you up. Seated, A to ' are notes (Z / X octave), or play a MIDI keyboard. V switches the camera. Esc lets go of the mouse.
