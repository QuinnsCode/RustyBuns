# Room surface textures

Photo textures for the studio rooms' own surfaces (`../gear-kit.ts`'s `ROOM`), all from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com)
under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free to ship, no credit needed, credited anyway.
Each was taken from the asset's 1K JPG set and shrunk to 512 px (colour and roughness at JPEG quality 70, the
normal map, OpenGL style, at 65). Budget: 1 MB for the folder (now 0.45 MB, 14 files). The gear's surfaces are
the shared kit's (`packages/models/textures/`).

| folder | ambientCG asset | maps |
|---|---|---|
| floorboards | [WoodFloor051](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=WoodFloor051) | color, normal, rough |
| foam | [AcousticFoam003](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=AcousticFoam003) | normal, rough (the colour is the room's) |
| ceilingTile | [OfficeCeiling001](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=OfficeCeiling001) | color, normal, rough |
| drywall | [PaintedPlaster017](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=PaintedPlaster017) | color, normal, rough (plaster too) |
| concrete | [Concrete034](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete034) | color, normal, rough |
