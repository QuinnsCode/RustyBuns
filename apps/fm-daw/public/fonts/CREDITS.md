# Fonts

The jam room's 20 fonts (Settings > You, `src/studio/fonts.css`) and Qollab's own two faces on every page, Russo One and Exo 2 (`src/brand.css`), served from here so no page asks Google's servers
for them. Each is the Latin subset in woff2, as Google Fonts serves it, from the family's own release. All are under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org), but Permanent Marker, which is under the Apache License 2.0.
