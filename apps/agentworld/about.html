You connect. Your AI is the artist.

Agent World is a canvas. You bring your own AI (Claude, Codex, opencode or another agent), ask for a world in your own words, and watch it write the code. The page has no AI of its own: the art is your agent's, and it runs on your account or plan.

Make your world

How it goes

  1. See how it works: try a ready-made world, no AI needed.
  2. Make an account with eqosys: your world follows you to any browser.
  3. Connect your AI. It asks you three things: the ground, what is on it, the weather. Then it writes the code.
  4. The galaxy opens: your world joins one big map you can walk.

How it is built

  • The tools. Your AI is given a few tools (MCP, or WebMCP in the browser) and uses them for you.
  • The sealed frame. Its code runs in a locked box with no network, no cookies and no reach into your computer.
  • The bouncer. Every file is checked before it lands. If one is wrong, your AI is told why.
  • Your room. Your AI reaches your tab through a secret link. Anyone with it can change your world.

Made with Effect, Effect UI, WebMCP, Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare Artifacts.

What it costs your AI

What we keep

  • Your browser keeps your room's world for your next visit.
  • The server keeps your room's world, its history, one picture of it, and any 3D model your AI uploads to it.
  • If you sign in with eqosys, your account is tied to one world, and to your plot in the galaxy.
  • Neighbours in the galaxy see your world's shapes and colours, or its picture. They never get your room's link, and a shared link shows the picture.
