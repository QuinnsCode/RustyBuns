# Gear surface textures

Photo textures for the shared material kit (`src/surfaces.ts`), all from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com) under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free to ship, no credit needed, credited anyway.
Each was taken from the asset's 1K JPG set and shrunk to 512 px (colour and roughness at JPEG quality 70, the
normal map, OpenGL style, at 65). Budget: 2 MB for the whole folder (now 1.8 MB, 26 files).
