Sign-in to Party to Game

From "who are you?" to "you are in seat 2", in three handoffs.

Three handoffs and no dropped batons. We checked. Twice.

This talk joins talks 6 and 7. One journey, start to finish. Go slowly on slide 6, the ticket.
The picture first

A night at the theatre

  1. The box office checks who you are and gives you a ticket.
  2. The lobby is where you meet your friends and wait together.
  3. The usher reads your ticket and walks your group to your seats.

eqosys is the box office. Qollab is the lobby. The game is the show. Nobody has to show ID three times.

The whole trip

Three stops

Stop 1 · the box office

The game sends you to sign in, and you come back

  • You press "Sign in" in the game.
  • The game sends you to eqosys. You sign in there, never in the game.
  • eqosys sends you back with a short code that works for one minute.
  • The game swaps the code for a ticket. Only the game that asked can swap it.

The game never sees your password. It cannot lose what it never had.

The "only the game that asked" part is a standard sign-in safety step. Name no standard and no fields.

Until when

The cute specific

A ticket anyone can check, and nobody can fake

eqosys signs every ticket with a secret stamp. It shows everyone the matching stamp checker.

  • A game room in the cloud checks the stamp itself.
  • It never phones eqosys to ask "is this real?".
  • So eqosys can be busy, or far away, and the game still lets you in.

Like a hologram on a banknote. The shopkeeper checks it at the till. Nobody calls the bank.

This is the "cute specific": a signed token the game checks on its own, with a public key. Do not name the algorithm or the claims.
Stop 2 · the lobby

Your ticket opens the lobby

  • With the ticket, the game connects you to your hub. Your friends see you are on.
  • You make a party. You get a six-letter code.
  • Friends join with the code, or with one tap on your name.
  • Everyone picks a seat and presses ready.

The lobby is where you find out your friend has been "five minutes away" for twenty minutes.

Stop 3 · the usher

Start hands everyone the same room

  • The host presses start.
  • Qollab tells every player the same thing: this room, your seat.
  • Each player's game joins that room. The room checks each ticket.
  • From here, the game runs the game. Qollab steps out.

The usher does not stay for the show. Ushers have seen it.
