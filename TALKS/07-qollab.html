Qollab

Friends, parties and spaces: the multiplayer every game ends up building, built once.

Every game studio has written a lobby. Every one of them swore it would be the last.

eqosys is who you are. Qollab is who you are with. Qollab is built into eqosys, and is also its own product.
The picture first

The phone lines between houses

Every house has its own rooms and its own rules. The phone lines join the houses.

  • Each game is a house. It keeps its own world.
  • Qollab is the lines: who is home, who is calling, who wants to come over.
  • When friends arrive, the house takes over. Qollab steps back.

The phone company does not care what you cook for dinner. It just makes sure your friends can find the door.

Four pieces

Hub, party, space, saves

Hub

Your own spot. Friends find you there, and see what you are doing now.

Like: your front porch light.

Party

A short-lived room with a six-letter code. Read it out, and friends join.

Like: a table at a café.

Space

A group that stays, with its own chat rooms and voice.

Like: a club with a clubhouse.

Saves

Your saves, kept for you and never read.

A party

Make, share, ready, go

  1. Make a party. You get a code like KXT7RP.
  2. Share it. Say it out loud, or send it.
  3. Ready up. Everyone takes a seat and presses ready. Chat while you wait.
  4. Go. The host starts. Everyone gets the same game room and their own seat.

The code has no vowels. That is so it never spells a rude word in front of your gran.

The no-vowels rule is the "cute specific" for this talk. Codes also expire after a day.
What stays with the game

Qollab gets you in the door. Then it leaves.

  • The game loop, the physics and the netcode never go through Qollab.
  • The game's own things, like decks, levels and items, stay in the game.
  • So Qollab never slows a game down, and a game can change engines without asking.

A good host introduces you, refills your drink and leaves you alone. Qollab has read the etiquette books.

How it is built

One small memory for each thing

Not one giant switchboard. A lot of tiny ones, each minding its own business.

Say what each piece does, not how they talk to each other.
Safe by default

Friends only, unless you say so

  • Rooms are for friends by default.
  • Chat has a speed limit, so nobody can flood it.
  • Mute, kick and report are built in, in every game.
  • Delete your account, and it reaches every room you were in.

Every game gets the same safety rails, so no game forgets them on a Friday afternoon.

Two products, one piece

Inside eqosys, and on its own

  • Every one of our games gets Qollab with the account. Sign in once, and your friends and party follow you from game to game.
  • Other developers could use Qollab alone, with their own sign-in, in their own cloud account.

Like the phone network. You can have the phone, or just the lines.
