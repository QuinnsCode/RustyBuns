eqosys

One account for every app. Your games, saves, achievements and friends, in one home.

Say it "eco-sis". Or don't. It answers to anything, as long as you sign in.

eqosys is who you are. Qollab (talk 7) is who you are with. Keep the two apart all talk.
The picture first

A library card for every branch

One card. It works in every library in town.

  • Every branch knows your name the moment you show it.
  • What you borrowed, and what you finished, follows you around.
  • Lose the card? Your history is not on the card. It is safe at the main desk.

Except this library also keeps your high scores. Libraries should really consider it.

What it holds

Five things, one account

Sign-in

Email and password, or a big sign-in button you already use. Add more than one, and every device opens the same account.

Your library

Every game you play, the one you played last, and what to pick up again.

Saves

Your game saves in the cloud. The last few copies are kept, so a bad save is not the end.

Achievements

Badges from every game, on one shelf. Friends see how many you have.

Friends

Who you play with, and what they are playing now.

Friends and parties are drawn by Qollab. eqosys holds the account they belong to.
Getting in

A friend gets in with one link

  • You send a friend your link.
  • They open it. If they have no account, they make one on the spot.
  • One tap, and you are friends. No email, no request to wait on.
  • Opening the link alone makes nobody your friend. They still have to say yes.

The best sign-up form is one your friend fills in because you dared them.

Saves

Saves we keep, but never read

  • A game hands eqosys a save. eqosys keeps it, sealed.
  • It never opens it. It only knows how big it is and when it came.
  • Two devices saved at once? The game can show you both and let you pick.

A coat check for your progress. We give you a ticket. We do not go through the pockets.

Your data, your call

Privacy you can press

  1. Download it. One button gives you everything eqosys holds about you, in one file.
  2. Kept near you. Your data stays in your part of the world.
  3. Delete it. One button removes the account, and every room and friend hears you left.

Leaving should be as easy as arriving. Ideally less paperwork.

For the people who make games

The studio: make, keep and ship

  • The same account that plays also makes.
  • Games from other studios can join the library, after a review.
  • A player can report a bug from inside any game. It lands straight on our to-do list, with no email on it.

The report button is the most honest critic we have. It is also the most polite.

For a game

A game gets all of this by asking

A game does not build its own sign-in, saves or friends. It asks eqosys.

  • A few lines in the game's page, and the player can sign in.
  • The same few lines give saves, achievements and a report button.
  • The game never sees a password.

Every game used to build a lobby, a login and a save system. Then a bug in each. Now they build the game.
